About Our School

Government Upper Primary School, Karlajori Antapali

Government Upper Primary School, Karlajori Antapali, established in 1946, is committed to providing quality, inclusive, and value-based education. We promote digital learning, creativity, scientific thinking, and holistic development to nurture confident, responsible, and future-ready citizens. Together, we strive to make our school a centre of excellence and innovation.
